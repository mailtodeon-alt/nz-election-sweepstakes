How scoring works

  • Party-vote bands: exact band 10 points, the next band along 4.
  • Parties in Parliament: exact 10, next band 5.
  • Most seats is 5. National + ACT + NZ First over 61 is 15. Highest minor is 10.
  • A missing result stays pending. It is not scored as zero.
  • If you tie, the closer guess at National’s exact party vote wins.
